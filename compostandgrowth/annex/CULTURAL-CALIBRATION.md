## Purpose 🌍

DIVINE is designed to measure and support Ψ across individuals and groups.  
**Culture profoundly shapes how Ψ manifests**, how observers interpret it, and how practices are received.  

Cultural Calibration ensures **accuracy, relevance, and ethical sensitivity** when applying DIVINE in diverse settings.

---

## 1. Why Culture Matters 🧠💡

- Norms and values shape **emotional expression (L)**  
- Social structures affect **interpersonal resonance (I)**  
- Collective expectations influence **systemic stress (S)**  
- Spiritual or ritual frameworks affect **EV and Σ perception**  

> Ignoring culture risks misinterpreting Ψ, harming participants, or observer burnout.

---

## 2. Core Principles of Cultural Calibration ⚖️

1. **Observe First, Apply Later**  
   - Spend time understanding the community’s social norms, language, and rhythms  
   - Do not impose standard protocols without adaptation  

2. **Language Sensitivity**  
   - Use terms the community understands  
   - Avoid abstract labels like “high Ψ” or “low L” in initial interactions  
   - Translate concepts into lived experience  

3. **Ritual & Temporal Alignment**  
   - Align observation schedules with local routines  
   - Respect religious, seasonal, and social cycles  

4. **Participatory Approach**  
   - Engage local members in designing protocols  
   - Feedback loops improve relevance and safety  
   - Collaborative design reduces observer-participant power imbalances  

5. **Ethical Anchoring**  
   - Cultural adaptation must **never compromise safety or consent**  
   - Maintain DIVINE’s ethical guardrails regardless of context  

---

## 3. Calibration Methods 🛠️

### a. Pre-Observation Scouting
- Interviews with local leaders or knowledgeable members  
- Identify triggers, stressors, and culturally-specific coping mechanisms  

### b. Pilot Observations
- Short, low-stakes sessions  
- Compare observations with local interpretations of wellbeing  

### c. Iterative Adjustment
- Modify instruments, protocols, and language  
- Include local indicators of flourishing alongside DIVINE measures  

### d. Documentation
- Maintain detailed notes on cultural variations  
- Helps future observers interpret Ψ without bias  

---

## 4. Special Considerations 🌱

- **Power Dynamics:** Be aware of hierarchy, gender, age, and social roles  
- **Privacy Norms:** Not all cultures allow open sharing of emotional or mental states  
- **Symbolic Practices:** Rituals or community ceremonies may affect EV and Σ readings  
- **Communication Style:** Direct vs indirect communication alters observer interpretation  

> Cultural calibration is **not optional**; it is essential for valid and ethical Ψ assessment.

---

## 5. Continuous Reflection 🔄

Observers should keep a **calibration journal**:

- What surprised me about local expression of Ψ?  
- Which assumptions did I carry that were challenged?  
- How did my presence affect the field?  
- What adaptations improved accuracy and safety?
