- **EV**: Existential value alignment 🌌

## 2. Daily DIVINE Check-In 🗓️💫

### Minimum Daily Essentials 🟢
- [ ] 7+ hours sleep 😴  
- [ ] 3 meals (simple OK) 🍎🥘  
- [ ] 10 min outside 🌞  
- [ ] 1 person aware you exist (text, call, proximity) 📱💌  
- [ ] Body check-in: "Am I breathing? Am I here?" 🫀

> **Note:** Only do more if it feels safe. No pressure.  

---

### Morning Ritual 🌅 (10 min)
1. Wake up without alarm if possible ⏰  
2. Lie still for 2 min 🛌  
3. Notice: “I am alive. I am breathing.” 🌬️  
4. Stand slowly, feet on floor 🦶  
5. Water. Window. Daylight on face 💧☀️  

---

### Evening Ritual 🌙 (15 min)
1. Screens off 30 min before sleep 📵  
2. Warm shower/bath 🛁  
3. Write 3 things that happened (no judgment) ✍️  
4. Lie down. Hand on heart 🫶  
5. Say: “I made it through today.” 🌟

## 3. Micro-Creativity Phase 🎨🪄 (Weeks 3-4)

> Warning: Avoid big creative leaps. Small novelty is safe.  

**Micro-S Activities:**
- Doodle one line 🖊️  
- Hum a sound (not a song) 🎶  
- Rearrange 3 objects on desk 🗂️  
- Change walking route home 🚶‍♀️  
- Wear something unusual 👗🧢  
- Cook one meal slightly differently 🍳  

**Journaling Prompt:**  
> “If I were even more alive inside, what would I want to do?”  
> Write freely. Delete if you want. Just get it out. 📝  

---

## 4. Rebuilding Relations 🤝💌 (Weeks 4-6)

**Low-Stakes Social:**
- Coffee with one person (30 min max) ☕  
- Walk with someone 🚶‍♂️  
- Join class (talk optional: yoga, painting) 🧘‍♀️🎨  
- Online community (lurk first) 💻  

**Vulnerability Gradients:**
- Level 1 (safest): "I've had it tough."  
- Level 2: "I've experienced something like collapse."  
- Level 3: "I couldn’t function for a while."  
- Level 4 (trusted only): Full story  

> Start at Level 1. Go deeper only if safe. 🛡️

## 8. Professional Help ⚕️🆘

DIVINE complements, doesn’t replace therapy. Seek help if:  
- Suicidal thoughts/plans 💔  
- Cannot meet basic needs >1 week 🥘🛁  
- Escalating substance use 🍷💊  
- Psychosis/dissociation  
- Self-harm or total isolation >1 month  

**Options:** EMDR, Somatic Experiencing, IFS, medication, outpatient, hospitalization
