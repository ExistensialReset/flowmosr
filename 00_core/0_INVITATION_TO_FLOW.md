# INVITATION TO FLOW

You may find it difficult to place Flow.

That is not so strange.

It touches on a whole range of different subjects and ideas that have been written and thought about throughout history. You may recognize parts of Flow from things you have already read, heard, or thought about yourself. Other parts may feel completely unfamiliar.

I myself find it a little difficult to explain exactly what Flow really is.

But you could say that Flow is a kind of social architecture, both from a large-scale and a small-scale perspective.

It is about how people can live together. About what conditions need to exist for life, freedom, creativity, curiosity, and participation to have room to flourish. About how we organize things, how power works, how information is allowed to move, how we handle conflicts, how we protect people, and how our structures can change when reality shows us that they do not work.

Flow is not something you need to join.

You do not need to have everything figured out.

You do not need to already live in a certain way.

You do not need to agree with what is written here.

But if you want to live in Flow, you may find yourself reconsidering old “truths” or changing certain things about how you see the world.

Not because Flow requires it of you.

But because a genuine investigation sometimes leads somewhere you did not expect to go in the beginning.

You may read Flow and recognize yourself in it.

You may think that some of the ideas are obvious.

You may become curious.

You may feel provoked.

You may think something is completely wrong.

You may not understand what I am doing at all.

All of that is allowed.

You can completely reject Flow.

My honest wish is simply that you become curious enough to read a little further.

Maybe you will find a thought that you find interesting and begin using it to think for yourself.

Maybe something in your understanding of reality will begin to shift.

Maybe you will discover that an idea has consequences for how people could live together.

Maybe you will investigate it further.

Maybe you will discover that the structure does not hold.

Maybe you will discover that it does.

Maybe something will change in the way you yourself want to live.

Maybe not.

That is enough.

Flow is not intended as a finished truth that you are supposed to accept.

It is an attempt to investigate how people could live together in other ways.

Here you will find thoughts.

Principles.

Boundaries.

Structures.

Protocols.

Questions.

And attempts that may still be wrong.

Read.

Investigate.

Does it hold?

What happens if it actually works?

What happens if it does not?

What needs to change?

What have I missed?

What does reality have to say?

And what happens when reality meets the model?

You do not need to arrive at any particular conclusion.

You do not need to become part of anything.

You do not need to call yourself anything.

But perhaps you will find something you did not see before.

And perhaps a thought of your own will begin to move.

That is the developmental spiral I hope Flow can set in motion:

**thought → investigation → changed understanding → new question → investigation → changed understanding → …**

It does not have to lead to Flow.

It does not even have to lead to you agreeing with Flow.

But perhaps it will lead somewhere.

That is really my honest wish with this invitation:

**that you want to read a little further.**

If you are not interested in reading more, I still thank you for taking the time to read through this invitation.

All the best to you who are reading this.

And if you want to — do not be afraid to reach out with a thought.
