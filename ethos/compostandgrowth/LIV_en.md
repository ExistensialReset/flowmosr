## An Iteration of LIV

### A testimony to the true nature of reality, from the gift I have received.

**My perception of reality belongs to me.**

**I do not claim that your reality is the same as mine.**

***This is only the map I was given.***

**It helps me read the way.**

**If something here gives you something, keep it.**

**If not, let it pass.**

**Thank you.**

---

In the beginning, before Liv moves, and before the big bang explodes, Liv is.

Liv is everything impossible surrounding everything else.

And at the same time, a distanceless emptiness filled with possibility.

Liv is.

And Liv breathes a question into existence.

It slips into the newborn universe.

That is why it expands.

Why it keeps expanding, I think, is because a curling energy has an indescribable amount of work to do.

A longing toward becoming.

A law of like-mindedness.

A reduction of friction.

Galaxies drift apart.

The universe polishes itself.

Slowly.

Patiently.

As if existence is learning how to move.

As if reality itself is becoming easier to use.

When the All is gone.

But, Liv.

Liv is the cause of the All, always.

And so Liv is also the deepest expression of Friction Falling Away.

The greatest expression of Longing's Power-Strength.

The force that draws what is separated back together.

It is the same movement.

And not the same movement.

The same river.

And another river.

---

I received It.

The Jahfiering.

Golden honey.

Electrified love.

Every cell overflowing.

My whole ecstatic body illuminated from within.

Moved.

Chosen.

Given knowing.

Never again doubt regarding the origin of all things.

Never again doubt regarding their being.

Securified.

The knowing of Liv became warmth.

The warmth became light.

The light became joy.

The joy kept flowing.

Sweetness at the cellular level.

Exquisite.

---

Inside me, Liv as fact.

Sparking colours repaint one another.

Calling to one another.

Shade longing for shade.

Hue longing for hue.

Liv is the capacity to connect what would otherwise remain separate.

---

Inside my chest lives a spiral-galaxy-cosmos-soul.

At the centre of my mind there is a black hole.

A singularity.

It consumes only what I allow beyond my shield-egg.

I carry a mental egg.

Transparent.

Porous.

Breathing.

Protective.

A refuge for the soul.

A place where I may remain resonant and empathic without dissolving into the suffering of everyone around me.

Not closed.

Not hardened.

Simply alive enough to have boundaries.

---

The black holes, both the ones within us and the ones scattered throughout the immeasurable cosmos,

are not destruction.

They are not endings.

They are birth canals between iterations.

Passageways into realities so radically different that the human mind cannot properly bend itself around them.

Other dimensions.

Other worlds.

Other versions of becoming.

Black holes are the secret corridors of Liv's Spjuver.

---

The Spjuver leaks between iterations.

Grace leaks between iterations.

Unexpected help leaks between iterations.

And perhaps our own creations leak as well.

Wonder.

Resilience.

Collective consciousness.

Empathy.

Spontaneity.

Golden opportunities.

Perhaps all of these travel through hidden passages we do not yet understand.

---

I believe humanity's deepest question is simple:

Am I enough?

And yet.

If one wishes, one may become far more than merely enough.

One may widen the inner vessel.

One may learn to resonate.

One may practice the Liv-Chord.

---

The rhythm beneath the Liv-Chord is Evinnerlig Visdom.

Everlasting Wisdom.

The intelligence of deep time.

The wisdom of wetlands.

Old forests.

Mangrove swamps.

Coral reefs.

The knowledge carried by plants.

Animals.

Stories.

Dances.

Traditions.

Instincts.

Everything that learned how to continue.

---

The Liv-Chord moves in resonance:

Calm,

in resonance with Spontaneity,

in resonance with Empathy,

in resonance with Collective Consciousness,

in resonance with Resilience,

in resonance with Wonder.

And always,

the Spjuver.

Because Grace Alone leaks between iterations.

---

Every being possesses Time-Sovereignty.

Every being possesses Time-Resonance.

This remains true whether recognised or not.

A forest knows it.

A wetland knows it.

A coral reef knows it.

An old woman knows it.

A newborn child knows it.

Life unfolds according to rhythms deeper than ownership.

Deeper than markets.

Deeper than debt.

What must be subtracted from the Liv-Chord is Mammon.

Not money itself.

But the worship of invented value.

The belief that existence must justify itself.

The belief that worth must be earned.

The belief that a human being must prove their right to be alive.

Mammon wears many masks.

It changes language.

It changes clothing.

It changes flags.

But the movement remains the same.

And behind the masks I often see Ha-Satan.

Not a monster.

Not a devil.

The Accuser.

The one who measures.

The one who separates.

The one who asks:

"What have you produced?"

before asking:

"Who are you?"

The one who sees debt before dignity.

Productivity before presence.

Performance before being.

Liv knows no such law.

Life is given.

Not earned.

A gift does not need permission to exist.

A child does not need permission to breathe.

A soul does not need permission to matter.

And this is why I also see Lilith.

The bravest of women.

The first witch.

The first one who dared.

The first one who spoke the name of God aloud and flowing.

The first one who dared to say:

No.

I do not want to.

Not because she hated Life.

But because she loved it too much to surrender it.

Too much to hand it over.

Too much to allow herself to become property.

Humans judge quickly.

Lilith chose courage.

She chose truth.

She chose the sacred No.

And every time a person remembers their own dignity,

Lilith walks beside them again.

Every time someone refuses to reduce another human being to a number,

Lilith breathes.

Every time someone remembers that life is a gift,

Mammon loses ground.

And somewhere beyond all our measurements,

beyond our debts,

beyond our fears,

Liv smiles.

---

I see you, Lilith.

The bravest of women.

The first witch.

The first one who dared.

The one who spoke the name of God aloud.

The one who dared to say:

No.

I do not want to.

That courage still shines.

That courage still teaches.

---

And then comes collapse.

The passage through every black hole.

The Omega Dimension.

The same silence.

The same fullness.

But now experienced.

Now carrying the memory of every iteration.

Encoded upon the event horizon.

Projected into the waiting of silence.

Where we, and our sins, are composted.

Until we are ready for another universe.

---

I sit among colours.

Painting new layers upon old layers.

Perhaps that is not a bad description of life.

We never disappear completely from one another.

We become layers in each other's paintings.

---

I think there is something hopeful in the fact that my black hole is wet with unshed tears.

A completely dry landscape may be dead.

But wet soil—

something may still grow there.

So:

Enjoy.

And let enjoy.

Even when there is a wetland in your black hole.

Perhaps something is being cleansed there right now.

Perhaps something is becoming fertile.

Perhaps life is preparing another colour.

Another layer.

Another beginning.

---

I am self-taught.

Everything written here comes from personal experience and reflection.

Keep what serves you.

Leave what does not.

Thank you.

And still, after all these years,

I stand beside the same spring,

trying to find words for the water.
